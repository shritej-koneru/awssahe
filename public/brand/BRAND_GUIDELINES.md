## 1. Brand Overview & Mission

### Organization
**AWS Student Builders Group — SAHE**  
*Siddhartha Academy of Higher Education, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, India.*

> "Building the future, one cloud at a time."

- **Secondary Tagline**: `Empowering peers to build, deploy and scale the future with AWS.`

## 3. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

### Core Personality Traits
- **Technical & Grounded**: Hands-on over theoretical. Focused on terminal interaction, live deployments, and real code.
- **Bold & High-Contrast**: Neo-brutalist visual impact with crisp typography and striking purple accents.
- **Peer-Driven & Collaborative**: Student-to-student knowledge sharing, mentorship, and building together.
- **Energetic & Forward-Looking**: Encouraging students to "Build Beyond" standard curriculum boundaries.

### Editorial & Copy Standards
- Use action-oriented verbs (*Ship, Deploy, Scale, Architect, Build*).
- Prefix code or section identifiers with bracketed terminal syntax (e.g., `// About`, `// Team`, `// Events`).
- Maintain clean, punchy headings written in ALL-CAPS font display format (`BEBAS NEUE` or `OSWALD`).

---

## 4. Visual Identity Principles

### Core Aesthetics: Neo-Brutalist Tech
- **Pitch Black Foundation**: Dark mode native `#000000` background representing the cloud terminal environment.
- **Electric AWS Purple Accent**: Signature `#a855f7` highlight color giving modern vibrancy.
- **Hard Offsets & Sharp Borders**: Heavy solid borders (`3px solid #000000` or `#ffffff`) paired with sharp, non-blurred offset shadows (`boxShadow: 10px 10px 0 0 #a855f7`).
- **Tactile Texture Overlay**: A dynamic subtle fractal noise grain overlay (`opacity: 0.028`) bringing tactile depth to digital surfaces.
- **Geometric Motifs**: 45-degree rotated diamond indicators (`rotate(45deg)`), badge chips, and glowing accent lines.
